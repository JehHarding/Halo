FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about using Halo. Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch and we'll help.

Getting started
What is Halo?

Halo is an app that helps you keep track of your pet's medications, vitals, and vet appointments, and share a clear care guide with anyone looking after them. It's built for pet owners managing an ongoing condition, a senior pet, or anyone who wants real peace of mind.

Can I use Halo for more than one pet?

Yes. Each pet gets their own complete profile, medications, vitals, contacts, calendar, and carer's pack, all in one place.

Is Halo free to use?

Yes. The free tier covers one pet, up to two medications with reminders, up to two upcoming calendar events, and unlimited vitals logging. Halo Plus removes those limits for only £5.99/month.

Does Halo replace my vet?

No. Halo is an information management tool, not a veterinary service. It does not provide veterinary advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified vet for your pet's health, and in an emergency, contact your vet or an emergency clinic directly rather than relying on the app.

My pet is healthy. Do I still need Halo?

Halo works just as well for healthy pets, vaccination records, vet contacts, routine reminders, and a carer's pack ready for whenever you travel. The peace of mind isn't only for complex cases.

Medications & reminders
How do medication reminders work?

Each medication gets its own schedule: daily, weekly on a specific day, monthly, a custom interval (like every 3 days), or as-needed with no reminder at all. You can add as many reminder times as a medication needs, for example twice daily at different times.

How many medications can I track for free?

Up to two active medications with reminders on the free tier. Halo Plus removes that limit. If you're already over the limit, nothing you've entered is hidden or deleted, you just won't be able to add a new one until you upgrade.

What if I miss a dose?

You can mark a dose as given at any time from the Medications tab. Halo doesn't penalise or flag missed doses, it's there to help you remember, not to judge.

Halo Plus & billing
What does Halo Plus include?

Unlimited medications, unlimited calendar events, full vitals history and trend charts, and PDF export of your Carer's Pack, for £5.99/month.

How do I subscribe or cancel?

Halo Plus is managed through the App Store, the same place as any other subscription on your device. You can cancel anytime and you'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

Will I lose data if I cancel Halo Plus?

No. Nothing is deleted. If you have more than one pet, you'll only see your first pet until you resubscribe, the rest are hidden, not removed, and reappear the moment you're back on Plus. Your existing medications, vitals, and calendar entries stay exactly as they are. You'll simply be limited to the free tier's caps for adding new entries going forward.

Privacy & data
Where is my pet's data stored?

On your device only, right now. We're building optional private iCloud sync so your data can back up and follow you across devices. Halo does not share, transmit, or sell your pet's information to anyone.

Who can see my Carer's Pack?

Only whoever you choose to share it with. On the free tier you can view the full guide in-app anytime. Halo Plus lets you export it as a PDF and send it to a sitter, family member, or vet, choosing exactly what's included.

Is Halo registered with the ICO?

Yes, Halo is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what data we collect and how it's protected.

Troubleshooting
My medication reminders aren't going off

Check that Halo has notification permission: Settings app > Halo > Notifications > Allow Notifications. Also make sure Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing the app, and that the medication's schedule and time are set correctly in the Medications tab.

I upgraded to Halo Plus but the app still shows free-tier limits

Open Settings and check the Halo Plus status. If it still shows as inactive, use "Restore Purchases" in Settings, this re-links your existing App Store subscription to the app without charging you again.

Will my data sync across devices?

Not currently. Halo stores everything on-device only, so switching phones or reinstalling the app means starting fresh. Use the Carer's Pack export to keep an outside copy of your pet's key details.
