# Sleeping breathing rate Reel (2026-09-28)

## Instagram / Facebook
Angel's sleeping breathing rate is one of the most useful numbers I track. Vets often ask owners to count it at home.

Count breaths for 15 seconds, multiply by 4. Under 30 a minute while asleep is normal. Consistently over 30, tell your vet.

Halo has a free breathing counter that does the maths and keeps the history for you. Link in bio 🐾
