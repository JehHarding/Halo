# Halo Content Plan — HeyGen avatar era
Started fresh 2026-08-17, replacing the veed-based plan.

Cadence: **2 avatar videos a week.**

## The format, unchanged from what already worked

The one post that's confirmed to drive saves, not just likes, was teaching a real skill (counting resting breaths) with a real person explaining it. Every video here follows that shape:

1. **~40-55s, avatar to camera**: teach the real skill, sourced from an existing blog post so there's no fresh writing needed
2. **Bridge line**: "...and Halo does this bit for you"
3. **~15-20s cutaway**: Simulator screen recording of the feature doing exactly what was just taught

## Why HeyGen changes the production notes

- Avatar can be created from as little as a 15-second reference video
- **Outfit isn't locked** — HeyGen's "Looks" feature lets a new short video or even just photos create a different outfit/setting for the same avatar, at no extra cost within your plan's avatar slot. Worth recording 2-3 outfits up front so videos don't look identical week to week.
- HeyGen has a **native screen recorder** built into its editor plus a dedicated **Avatar Positioning Tool** (scale, offset, dimensions, live preview) — record the Halo app footage and composite the avatar into a corner window inside HeyGen itself, no second tool needed
- **Test the corner placement on one real render first.** Real users have reported the avatar's position in the editor preview doesn't always exactly match the final rendered video — check this once before queuing a whole batch of scripts assuming the preview is 1:1
- Credits: at ~40-55s of avatar speech per video, each video costs roughly 13-18 credits. Creator plan (600 credits/month) covers 2/week (~9 videos/month) many times over, so credits are not a constraint at this cadence
