Packing Your Pet's
Medication for
Christmas Away

4 min read

Going away for Christmas with a pet on medication takes a little planning, mostly because vets and pharmacies close. Do the checking now, not on Christmas Eve.

Quick answer
Take enough medication plus a few spare days, keep it in its labelled packaging, write the routine down, and find an out-of-hours vet near where you are staying.

Check the supply

Look at how much medication you have and when it runs out. Take enough for the whole trip plus a few spare days in case you are delayed. If it will run short, ask your vet for a repeat prescription well before the practice closes for the holidays.

Keep it in its packaging

  • Keep medication in its original labelled packaging, with the dose instructions.
  • Check storage. Some medicines need to be kept cool or away from sunlight.
  • Keep it in your hand luggage or the car cabin, not the boot.
  • Keep it out of reach of children and other pets where you are staying.

Write the routine down

Write out each medication, the dose and the times, and note anything that has changed recently. If someone else will be giving a dose, they should not have to guess. A written schedule also helps if you need to see a vet away from home.

Keep to the usual times

A busy day with visitors is exactly when a dose gets missed. Set phone alarms at the usual times, and keep the same routine as far as you can.

Know your plan B: save the number of the vet at home, and find an out-of-hours vet near where you are staying before you set off.
