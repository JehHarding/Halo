# UK veterinary cardiology practices

Compiled 2026-08-13. Use with the email in `VET-OUTREACH.md`.

## How to read this

Every practice below was confirmed in this session to run a cardiology referral service, from search results and their own published pages. Named cardiologists are included where a source named them.

**No email addresses are listed here, deliberately.** Guessing an address format is the fastest way to look like a spammer, and I will not invent one. Each of these publishes a contact page. Get the address from there, and use a named cardiology contact over a general enquiries inbox wherever one exists.

Verify the named clinicians before you write. Specialists move between practices often, and a first line addressed to someone who left eighteen months ago is worse than no first line at all.

## The argument, in one paragraph

Worth having straight in your head before you write to any of them.

Standard cardiology practice after a heart failure diagnosis is to send the owner home to record sleeping respiratory rate daily, on paper, in a booklet the practice provides. Published owner guidance already says that a diary or a computer database is an acceptable alternative. So Halo is not asking anyone to change their protocol or adopt anything new. It is the alternative their own guidance already permits, built properly. That is a much smaller thing to say yes to than a new product.
