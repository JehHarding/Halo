The problem

Your pet can't tell you
when something is wrong.
Every detail has to live in your head.

Three medications, a cardiologist's number, an implant, quarterly blood tests, an annual scan, an emergency contact list. It's a lot to carry. Let Halo carry it for you.

1.

Smart Pet
Profile

Stop re-explaining your pet's history at every appointment. Halo keeps their profile complete, shareable, and always up to date, for every vet, carer, and specialist who needs it at a glance. Looking after more than one? Add every pet and switch between them in a tap.

2.

Medication
Reminders

For pets on multiple medications, timing is everything. Halo gives you a precise daily schedule, tap-to-confirm logging and a full record of every dose, so nothing slips or gets forgotten.

3.

Health
Calendar

Every appointment, every contact, every reminder. All in one place. Because forgetting a blood test isn't an option.

4.

Vitals
Log

Breathing rate, weight, key metrics, all logged and charted over time. With a built-in 15-second counter, the exact method specialists ask for, so you walk into every appointment with the data that matters.

5.

Holiday
Carer's Pack

Going away shouldn't mean worrying the whole time. One tap sends your pet's complete care guide, medications, contacts, routine and emergency numbers to whoever is looking after them, so you can actually be present wherever you are.

So you can travel knowing whoever is caring for your pet has everything they need to do it exactly as you would.
