Questions Worth Asking
at Every Vet Appointment

4 min read

It's easy to walk out of a vet appointment having forgotten to ask the one thing you actually wanted to know. This is the list worth having ready before you go in.

About anything new or changed

  • Is this within a normal range for my pet specifically, not just the general guide?
  • Should I be tracking this at home, and if so, how often?
  • What would make this worth an unscheduled call rather than waiting for the next visit?

About any medication

  • What exactly does this do, and how will I know it's working?
  • What are the realistic side effects to watch for, not just the rare ones?
  • What should I actually do if a dose is missed, delayed, or given twice by accident?

About what's next

  • What should the next check-up specifically look for?
  • Is there anything worth tracking between now and then?
  • Given everything discussed today, is there anything you'd want to know sooner rather than waiting for the next scheduled visit?
Worth doing: bring an actual record, weight, breathing rate, daily notes, rather than trying to summarise weeks of observations from memory in the room. It changes the quality of the conversation.
This guide is for information only and isn't veterinary advice. Every appointment is different, ask whatever's actually on your mind, this is a starting point, not a limit.

Bringing the record, not the memory

The single biggest upgrade to a vet appointment isn't a better question, it's walking in with real data instead of an impression.
